How the Draw Works

  • Each participant ($30 entry) is randomly placed on a team.
  • Daily eliminations run Nov 21–27 (~50% drawn before party night), with the remaining names drawn live at the party until one name remains.
  • As long as someone on your team is still in, you're still in — the winning team splits all the wine equally.

Calgary Food Bank Fundraiser

About 25% of every entry goes to The Calgary Food Bank and 75% buys wine (over $2,500 raised in 2025). A non-perishable food hamper and cash donation box will be at the party, and the runner-up receives the donation tax receipt.
